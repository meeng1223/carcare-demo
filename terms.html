Emergency and roadside features

CarCare is not an emergency service

CarCare does not contact 911 or any emergency service for you, does not dispatch help, and cannot guarantee that an alert to your contacts will be delivered. It is not a substitute for calling 911. In an emergency, call 911 yourself.

  • Emergency alerts send your live location to the contacts you choose, when you trigger them. Delivery depends on your phone, network and location services.
  • Incident records are your own records. We make no promise that they will be enough for any police report, insurance claim or legal proceeding.
  • Roadside assistance helps you find and call your own roadside provider, insurer or warranty company, or a nearby business. CarCare does not provide roadside service, send anyone to you, or promise how fast help will arrive.
  • Emergency features are free on every plan.

Driving safely

Your attention belongs on the road. Don't read or type in the app while driving. Use Drive Mode, voice commands or CarPlay only when it's safe and legal where you are, and pull over for anything else. Follow all traffic laws. CarCare holds non-urgent notifications while Drive Mode is on, but you remain responsible for how you use your phone in the car.

Sharing a vehicle with your household

On Plus and Pro you can invite household members to share a vehicle. Each person keeps their own account, trips, expenses and tax figures. Sharing never combines them.

If more than one person logs business miles on the same vehicle in a tax year, each of you should claim only your own miles, and only one of you can use the actual-expense method for that vehicle. CarCare will remind you, but it doesn't decide who may claim what. Check with your tax professional.

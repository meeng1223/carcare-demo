About CarCare

We help drivers keep more of what their car earns them.

CarCare.help is the all-in-one car-ownership app from Car Registration Help Inc. in San Jose, California. It captures every mile, logs every expense, reminds you before service is due and finds any shop nearby, so your car's paperwork takes care of itself.

Why we started with mileage

Most drivers leave money on the road.

Self-employed and gig drivers under-claim an estimated 20 to 40 percent of their eligible business miles, mostly because keeping a log is tedious. So we built the app to keep the log for you. It should pay for itself, and it should be useful from the first drive, before we ask anything else of you.

What we believe

Six promises we built into the product.

These aren't just policies. Each one is enforced in how CarCare is built.

Your data isn't for sale

We earn money from subscriptions, not from selling where you drive or what you spend.

Safety is never behind a paywall

Emergency features are free on every plan, and we never show an upgrade prompt when you need help.

You decide what a place is

CarCare never guesses why you went somewhere. Medical and charity visits get extra protection.

We don't keep your voice

Speech becomes text and the audio is discarded. No always-on listening, ever.

Every shop on the map

We show every nearby shop, not just the ones that pay us, and searching is free.

Straight answers about money

Our numbers are estimates with sources, never guarantees. We'll tell you when to ask a professional.

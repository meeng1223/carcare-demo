        Questions

        Good to know.

        Does CarCare drain my battery?

        No. Trip capture is built to stay under 5% of daily battery. It wakes on motion, Bluetooth or CarPlay and sleeps when you're parked.

        Is my location private?

        Your places are labeled by you, never guessed. CarCare doesn't sell location data, and there's no always-on wake word listening in the background.

        Will my mileage log hold up in an audit?

        Each trip records the four things the IRS asks for: date, distance, destination and business purpose. Your Audit Confidence Score shows which trips are missing something, so you can fix them before tax time.

        I'm a W-2 employee. Is this for me?

        Yes. Employees can no longer deduct mileage, but most employers reimburse it. CarCare writes a reimbursement report in the format Concur, Expensify and Rippling accept.

        How long do you keep my records?

        Three years on every plan, including Free, which matches how long the IRS can usually look back.

        Can the AI advisor fix my car?

        It answers car questions like "what does P0420 mean?" and points you to the right kind of shop. For anything safety-critical it will always tell you to see a certified mechanic.
